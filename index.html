Privacy policy

This policy covers the Akashpatal app for iPhone and iPad.

Akashpatal does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not connect to the internet.

LAN party. If you choose to play LAN party, the app talks directly to the other phones and tablets in your squad over your own Wi-Fi network. That is why iOS asks for permission to use the local network. The join code shown on the host's screen is that device's address on your network. Nothing is sent outside your network, and nothing is sent to us.

The app saves three things on your device so the game remembers you between sessions:

  • your settings, such as steering, sound, aim assist and where you placed the buttons;
  • your career: banked coins, the ships you own, and your best runs;
  • which tutorial tips you have already seen.

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email abhayfirstranker@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

LAN party will not connect? Make sure every device is on the same Wi-Fi network and that Akashpatal is allowed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Then type the host's join code on the other devices.
